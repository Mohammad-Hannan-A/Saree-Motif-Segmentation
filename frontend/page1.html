Academic Principle: ITU-R BT.601 Weighted Luminance Grayscale Conversion

Color saree images contain three color bands: Red, Green, and Blue. Because human eyes are biologically most sensitive to green wavelengths and least sensitive to blue, a simple average (R+G+B)/3 creates unnatural contrast. GNU Octave computes the true perceptual grayscale intensity using:

Y = 0.2989 × R + 0.5870 × G + 0.1140 × B
