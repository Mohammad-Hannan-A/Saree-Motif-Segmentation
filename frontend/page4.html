Motif Extraction & Placement on Destination Images

Extract decorative patterns using Bounding Box cropping, Color thresholding, or Morphological Mask refinement. Export transparent 32-bit PNGs and composite motifs onto garments or everyday objects using GNU Octave.

Extraction Modes & Alpha Transparency Compositing

Bounding Box Mode: Crops strictly the selected rectangular Region of Interest (ROI) without resizing the full image. Mask-Based Extraction: Employs color thresholding and morphological hole-filling to generate an 8-bit Alpha matte, saving a genuine 32-bit transparent PNG. Compositing: GNU Octave applies Porter-Duff 'Over' alpha blending to seamlessly composite the motif onto another garment without modifying the original destination photo.
